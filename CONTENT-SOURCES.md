# Content provenance

Reviewed on 17 November 2026.

This project is an independent portfolio concept using product families and imagery publicly displayed by Al-Ahram for Factories Requirements. It is not presented as a newly authorized official company website.

## Company and contact information

- https://www.ahramvalves.com/Company — Egyptian ownership/administration, more than 40 years of exposure to Egypt’s process industry, technical discussions, site inspections and engineering support.
- https://www.ahramvalves.com/AR/index — company email `info@ahramvalves.com` and product terminology.
- https://www.ahramvalves.com/Contact — Cairo address and phone number. The English site contains a misspelled email domain; the Arabic site’s `info@ahramvalves.com` is used instead.

## Product families

- https://www.ahramvalves.com/products?i=1 — manual and automated valves, check valves, flow meters, piping/fittings and expansion joints.
- https://www.ahramvalves.com/products?i=1&n=2 — gate and globe valves.
- https://www.ahramvalves.com/AR/products?i=1&n=3 — soft seated ball valves and their product image.
- https://www.ahramvalves.com/products?i=6 — mechanical seals under rotating equipment.

Each product also includes a source URL in `src/lib/products.ts` and a source link on its detail page. No pressure, temperature, material, certification, stock, pricing, customer or testimonial claims have been invented. Descriptions guide visitors to confirm application details directly with the company.
